Abstract—- An array of Rectangular Microstrip Antenna 
(RMSA) is designed and compared with single patch RMSA 
and optimized for obse rving an increase in  directivity along 
with achieving expected radiatin g parameters such as S11 < -
10dB and Side Lobe Level (SLL) < -20dB using IE3D 
simulator. Maximum directivity of 13.058dBi is obtained by 
implementing four by two (4x2) array of eight patches as 
compared to 6.74dBi for single patch for the application of 
WLAN at 2.4 GHz centre frequency. The parametric variations 
are done for obtaining desired results.
 Keywords — RMSA, Four by Two Array (4x2), IE3D, FR4.
I. INTRODUCTION 
n antenna plays a very important role in wireless 
communication systems. For applications emerging of
compact size technologies, m icrostrip antennas are 
used because of their low cost, portability, versatile, 
conformal and other factors supporting the needs. But 
microstrip antennas also have  disadvantages like narrow 
bandwidth, low gain and excitation of unwanted surface 
waves. Hence antenna arrays ar e used to overcome for these 
disadvantages, thereby introducin g microstrip patch arrays. 
This technology of microstrip  arrays makes it possible to 
provide high gain, desired radiation pattern, appropriate 
beamwidth with least cost and less space. Recent advances 
have shown increase in the bandwidth and gain using 
microstrip array [1].
  For desired radiation pattern and directivity each element 
in the array can be fed in many ways ;    a) same amplitude 
and same phase difference b) same amplitude and varying 
phase difference c) varying amplitude and same phase 
difference and d) varying amplitude and varying phase 
difference. But uniform arrays are used which are easy for 
construction [2], [4]. 
  In this paper four by two (4x2) array, edge fed by a 
microstrip line for WLAN at 2.4 GHz is presented using 
inset feeding method in contrast to reference [2] where a four 
by one (4x1) array is designed for WiMAX application at 2.5 
GHz.
   Quarter wave transforme rs are used to match the 
impedance of the transmission lines considering SMA 
connector at the input to the antenna [4]. The resonant 
frequency is determined to be 2.4 GHz.   
  Using IE3D simulator, the op timized antenna array of 4x2 
patches gives a return loss (S11) of -19.64dB. Many 
optimizations are carried out by  for getting better expected 
results for gain and direc tivity. Next part explains the 
antenna designing procedure respectively. 
II. A NTENNA DESIGN
   Starting with the designing of a rectangular microstrip 
patch antenna, there are some  parameters chosen. Those 
are substrate related parameters such as its relative 
permittivity, loss tangent and height of the substrate. 
Available dielectric lies in the range from 2.2< ε r <12, and 
lower permittivity is  chosen for better performance [2] in 
terms of gain. FR4 substrate is chosen having εr as 4.3 and 
the loss tangent as 0.01 with 1.6mm as thickness as it is 
easily available. 
  A single patch is designe d for the given application and 
selected substrate. And consequently 4x2 array is designed 
for comparing its performance with that of single patch.

IV. CONCLUSION 
 A comparison is carried ou t between a single microstrip 
patch with the array of 4x2 (eight elements) in terms of 
important radiation parameters. The performance of the array 
outcomes the required results rather than the single patch for 
directivity, gain, return loss (S11) as well as the bandwidth. 
The desired requirements for 2. 4 GHz is satisfied by 4x2 
eight element patch array which gives return loss of about 
−19.64dB and a VSWR of 1.26 at the centre frequency of 
2.4GHz. Additionally, the performance of the array is 
optimized by adjusting the lengt h and width of each patch 
gives gain of 10.5dB relative to  3.7dB of single patch. It 
depends on other factors also such as feeding technique, 
substrate type, thickness of dielectric etc. respectively.
